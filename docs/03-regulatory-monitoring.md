# Regulatory monitoring and deadlines

LegalGuard helps the legal and compliance team keep track of regulatory changes and important deadlines. The regulatory watcher surfaces changes for review, while the compliance calendar and deadline alerts help keep follow-up on schedule.

## How it works

1. The watcher tracks regulatory updates relevant to the work.
2. A reviewer checks the change and its effect on the organization.
3. Confirmed obligations and important dates are added to the compliance calendar.
4. Deadline alerts remind the team to follow up.

Human review keeps the process grounded in the organization’s actual obligations. The reviewer confirms the change, due date and next step before acting on an alert.
