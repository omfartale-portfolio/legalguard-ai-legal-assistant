# Contract review and redlining

LegalGuard helps review contracts clause by clause. It highlights potential risks, provides clause-level suggestions and prepares redlines for review. Drafting can draw on a pre-approved clause library. The contract risk dashboard brings key findings together.

## How a review works

1. LegalGuard reads the contract and organizes the review around its clauses.
2. It flags terms that may need attention and suggests possible changes.
3. For drafting, it can use wording from the pre-approved clause library.
4. It prepares proposed edits as a redline and runs an auditor self-check. Legal answers can also be checked against the curated legal sources.
5. A human reviewer examines the findings and decides what to accept, revise or escalate.

## Example

A fictional services contract includes a short termination notice period. LegalGuard can flag the clause, explain why it may deserve attention and suggest that the reviewer compare it with the approved position. The reviewer checks the business context and chooses the final wording.

The goal is to make clause analysis and redlining faster to review while keeping legal judgment with the human reviewer.
