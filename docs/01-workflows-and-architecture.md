# How LegalGuard works

LegalGuard brings AI assistance and legal knowledge together to make legal review more accurate and cost-efficient. I designed the system using chatbot assistance and legal knowledge of my own.

## The workflow

1. **Review a contract.** LegalGuard examines clauses, identifies potential risks and offers clause-level suggestions. It can also draft from a pre-approved clause library and prepare redlines for review.
2. **Check legal answers.** An auditor pass provides a self-check, and a verification layer checks answers against 30–40 curated legal sources.
3. **Track regulatory changes.** The regulatory watcher helps surface relevant changes. The compliance calendar and deadline alerts help keep follow-up visible.
4. **Review and act.** The contract risk dashboard brings issues together. A person reviews the analysis and decides what to accept, change or escalate.

I have personally assessed every answer and found the results highly accurate in my use, particularly on dates and details that are easy to overlook or mishandle.

## Designed for practical legal work

LegalGuard is currently used for professional work. It is designed to provide reliable AI assistance at a cost-efficient level of maintenance. The product is not offered for sale or publicly released.

This repository describes the use cases and workflows while keeping the product’s private implementation details out of public view.
