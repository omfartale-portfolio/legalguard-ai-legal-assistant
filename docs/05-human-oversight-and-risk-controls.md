# Human oversight and risk controls

LegalGuard pairs AI assistance with human review. The system helps analyze contracts and legal questions, while people review the results and make the final decisions.

## The review loop

- **Contract suggestions:** A reviewer checks the clause, proposed change and business context before using a redline.
- **Legal answers:** The auditor pass and source verification provide additional checks. I personally reviewed every answer during my assessment of LegalGuard.
- **Regulatory alerts:** A reviewer confirms that a change applies and checks the relevant deadline before acting.
- **Risk dashboard:** Reviewers use the surfaced contract issues to prioritize follow-up.

This approach keeps human judgment at the center of contract review, legal research and compliance work, while LegalGuard helps make the review more focused and efficient.
