# Example workflows

These examples show how LegalGuard supports common legal and compliance tasks. All parties and contract details are fictional.

## Contract review

A reviewer opens a services agreement. LegalGuard reviews the clauses, flags a termination term that may need attention and suggests language from the pre-approved clause library. It prepares a redline for the reviewer to assess.

## Legal research

A reviewer asks a legal question. LegalGuard draws on the legal knowledge supplied to it and checks the answer against its curated legal sources. The reviewer checks the answer and its dates before relying on it.

## Regulatory monitoring

The regulatory watcher surfaces a change for review. A reviewer checks whether it applies, adds any confirmed date to the compliance calendar and follows up through deadline alerts.
